Yours alone: Kindoo records every action under your name, so it is as good as your password. Doorman keeps it on the server and never shows it back to you.
How to get one

Only the web version of Kindoo has it — the phone app does not — so this takes a computer. Sign in at web.kindoo.tech and leave that tab open.

The easy way. Drag this button up onto your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B shows the bar if it is hidden). Then, on the Kindoo tab, click it — it copies your token, ready to paste above. Keep it: you will want it again the next time Kindoo signs you out.

Copy my Kindoo token

By hand, if you would rather. On the Kindoo tab press F12, open Application (Firefox calls it Storage), expand Local Storage → https://web.kindoo.tech, and copy the value next to Token.

Either way it looks like 3f2504e0-4f89-11d3-9a0c-0305e82c3301 — 36 characters, four dashes. It keeps working for months, but signing out of Kindoo kills it, so close the tab instead. If Doorman starts saying it cannot reach Kindoo, come back here and paste a fresh one.
